ABOUT

Jonathon W. Marshall is an Ontario-based photographer working with film, digital photography, and handmade photographic prints.

His documentary practice follows body suspension communities, recording preparation, ritual, the experience of suspension, and the people who make it possible. His work extends from the camera to the darkroom through film development, printing, and cyanotype.
